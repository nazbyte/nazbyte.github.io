Apps I have taken from idea to store

Two products of my own — because the fastest way to prove I can ship is to ship.

Android In closed testing on Google Play React Native

Tasbeeh 33: Dhikr Counter

A dhikr counter built for people who count on their fingers and lose track. Coloured beads fill as you count, progress is never lost, and the whole app works offline. Morning and evening adhkar, after-salah adhkar and the daily dhikr are included.

  • Full design system and ten colour themes, built around a 33-bead ring
  • Offline-first counting and history — no account, no data collected
  • One-time unlock through Google Play Billing, no subscription
  • Reminders with exact alarm scheduling, plus a home-screen widget
  • 87 dhikr entries with their sources, in the app's own library
  • Complete store listing, data-safety and content-rating compliance, and the closed-testing programme
Android + iOS In design React Native + Skia

Elixir Sort

A glowing-liquid puzzle game: pour, sort, and get the colours to settle. Built on React Native with Skia for the liquid rendering — no physics engine, so it stays smooth on modest phones.

  • Level progression, hints and a relaxed no-timer mode
  • Custom shader work for the pour and the glow
  • Designed to run warm-launched with the user's first launch
